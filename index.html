About Us

Trusted Global Logistics Partner

UTi Logistics Vietnam was established on the foundation of more than 20 years of logistics industry experience gained through leading global logistics companies, including UTI Worldwide, DSV, DB Schenker, and WTC (World Trade Connection).

Our management team has held various key positions throughout the logistics supply chain, including Documentation Executive, Import & Export Specialist, Sales Executive, and Business Manager. Our Services

What We Offer For You

Air Freight

With a highly experienced team, we specialize in handling a wide range of cargo, including temperature-controlled shipments, dangerous goods (DG), high-value cargo, and other special commodities.

Supported by strong partnerships with major airlines, we are able to provide flexible air freight solutions—from urgent shipments requiring immediate uplift to cost-effective routing options for customers seeking optimized transportation costs. Whatever your requirements, we tailor our services to best meet your business needs.

Sea Freight

Through long-term partnerships with leading shipping lines and global consolidators, we provide comprehensive ocean freight services to ports worldwide.

Our services include both Full Container Load (FCL) and Less than Container Load (LCL) shipments for general cargo, refrigerated cargo, and dangerous goods (DG), ensuring reliable and competitive shipping solutions for every type of cargo.

Customs Brokerage

Our experienced customs brokerage team possesses extensive knowledge of various product categories and maintains an up-to-date understanding of Vietnam Customs regulations.

We provide professional customs clearance services for trading companies, logistics providers, manufacturers, and factories across Vietnam, ensuring smooth, compliant, and efficient import and export procedures.

Inland Transportation & Delivery

Our extensive fleet of trucks and tractor units, strategically positioned at major airports and seaports throughout Vietnam, enables us to respond quickly to customers' transportation needs.

We are committed to providing fast, reliable, and secure delivery services, ensuring that your cargo reaches its destination safely and on schedule.
